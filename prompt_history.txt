PROMPTS THAT BUILT THE PROGRAM
====================================================================

--- Prompt 1 · 30 September 2026 -----------------------------------
Attached: "OspanExempleFile.csv" (an empty template with the 13 OSPAN column names)

"gör en program i xcode som jag kan ladda upp all test data, lägger
ihop allt, och skapar en stor csv där vi kan lätt jämföra alla
resultat på ospan och deras tider"

[Make a program in Xcode where I can upload all the test data, that
merges everything and creates one big CSV where we can easily compare
all OSPAN results and their times.]

Result: the first version of the app.
  - Reads the OSPAN test's .csv files and the older .txt files, one
    file per participant, by drag and drop or from a whole folder.
  - Reads the condition (with phone / without phone) and the
    participant's name from the file name.
  - Flags files with identical data, incomplete runs and test runs,
    and leaves them out by default.
  - Shows one row per participant and a comparison of the two
    conditions (mean and standard deviation).
  - Exports three CSV files: one row per participant, all rows in long
    format, and the group comparison.


--- Prompt 2 · 30 September 2026 -----------------------------------
Attached: "Enkät om mobilanvändning (Exempel Svar).csv" (exemaple survey answers)

"Nu gör en flik där jag kan tolka datan från denna:
https://www.healthyscreens.com/sas-sv.pdf"

[Now make a tab where I can interpret the data from this one:
https://www.healthyscreens.com/sas-sv.pdf]

Result: a second tab, "Mobilanvändning (SAS-SV)".
  - Scores each survey answer 1–6 according to the Smartphone Addiction
    Scale – Short Version (Swedish and English answer labels).
  - Shows the total score and whether it is below or above the cutoff.
  - Links each survey answer to the OSPAN run saved closest in time.
  - Shows the scores per condition, the correlation with OSPAN and the
    mean per question.
  - Adds the SAS-SV columns to the CSV export.


--- Prompt 3 · 4 October 2026 --------------------------------------
Attached: "Enkät om mobilanvändning (Exempel Svar).csv" (exemaple survey answers)


"Jag skulle säga att SAS score verkar vara en dålig indikator på
presentand på Ospan testet - och det som hade bättre poäng var det
som mer "mobil beroende""

[I would say the SAS score seems to be a poor indicator of performance
on the OSPAN test – and those who scored better were the ones who
were more "phone dependent".]

Result: an analysis of the claim, and changes to the app so that it
reads the complete survey file correctly.
  - All 10 questions are read, with their question texts.
  - The cutoff is chosen by gender (31 for men, 33 for women).
  - The survey's own column with the OSPAN file name is used to link
    each answer to the right participant.
  - The survey's other number scales are no longer mistaken for SAS-SV
    questions.


--- Prompt 4 · 5 October 2026 --------------------------------------

"kan nu bygga en vy där vi kan expoerta olika grafer, stapeldiagram
och liknande baserat på datan?"

[Can you now build a view where we can export different graphs, bar
charts and similar, based on the data?]

Result: a third tab, "Diagram".
  - Six chart types: mean per group with error bars, box plot, one bar
    per participant, scatter plot with trend line, results per set
    size, and SAS-SV per question.
  - Choice of measure, grouping (condition, gender or SAS level),
    error bars, title and size.
  - Export as PNG or PDF, or copy to the clipboard.
  - A suggested figure caption and a table of the values behind each
    chart.


--- Prompt 5 · 5 October 2026 --------------------------------------
Attached: "Enkät om mobilanvändning (Exempel Svar v2).csv" (exemaple survey answers)

"kan du också ta med antal timmar skärmtid och utvildad och stress
faktorn"

[Can you also include the number of hours of screen time and the
rested and stress factors.]

Result: the survey's other number questions became measures of their
own: screen time, how often the participant thought about the phone
during the test, how rested and how stressed they felt before the test.
They appear in the survey tab, in the CSV export and as measures in the
charts. Screen time was answered in ranges and is counted as the
midpoint of the range.


--- Prompt 6 · 5 October 2026 --------------------------------------

"i graf vyn kan göra så att jag kan byta språk till engelska för
texterna i grafen (då presentation ska vara på engelska)"

[In the graph view, can you make it possible to switch the language to
English for the texts in the graph (since the presentation will be in
English).]

Result: a language switch in the chart tab (Svenska / English). In
English, the title, explanation, axes, group names, question labels,
number format and suggested caption are all in English.
